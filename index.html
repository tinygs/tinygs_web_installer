Welcome to the TinyGS web installer!

  1. Plug in your ESP to a USB port. We will install TinyGS 2509061 to it.
  2. Hit "Install" and select the correct COM port. No device found?
  3. Station Registration:
    1. First, join the Telegram group here.
    2. Then, open a private chat with @tinygs_personal_bot.
You will then choose one of two paths for configuration:

➡️ Path 1: Automatic Setup 🤖

After the installation is complete, click "NEXT" then "CONNECT TO WI-FI" to connect your device to your network.

Next, click "VISIT DEVICE" to get your One-Time Password (OTP). This password will also appear on your device's OLED screen.

Finally, ask your Telegram @tinygs_personal_bot for /weblogin to login to your TinyGS dashboard on the tinygs.com website and click "New Station." Follow the on-screen prompts, using the OTP you just obtained to configure your station.

➡️ Path 2: Manual Setup 🛠️

Ask tyour Telegram @tinygs_personal_bot to receive your MQTT credentials. You'll need these for the next step.

Once the firmware is installed and your board boots up for the first time, connect to the Wi-Fi Access Point named "My TinyGS".

Open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1 to access the configuration page. On this page, enter your home Wi-Fi details and the MQTT credentials you got from the bot.

Get TinyGS installed and connected in less than 5 minutes!
